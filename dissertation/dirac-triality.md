# Real Clifford Algebra, Split Octonions, and Triality in Signature (4,4)

## A reproducible exact and numerical study

### Abstract

This dissertation gives an exact-real construction of the Clifford algebra
`Cl(4,4)`, its spin group, the two real half-spin modules, split-octonion
multiplication, and split-real triality. The full Clifford algebra is realized
by eight integral 16 by 16 matrices and is proved to have rank 256. Its even
part preserves two rank-eight eigenspaces of the volume element. Direct
commutant and intertwiner calculations prove that these spaces are irreducible
and inequivalent as real `Spin(4,4)` modules.

An eight-dimensional split composition algebra is independently constructed
from Zorn data in an orthogonal basis of signature `(4,4)`. Its multiplication
is proved unital, alternative, and norm-composing. Passing to the para-product
produces a cyclic invariant trilinear form. The associated related-triple Lie
algebra is computed from 512 linear constraints on 84 parameters. It has
dimension 28; all three projections are isomorphisms, their eight-dimensional
representations are irreducible and pairwise inequivalent, and exact outer
operators generate a six-element permutation group.

Two pure-Rust SUNDIALS CVODE studies accompany the exact work. A 24-state
transport problem evolves the vector and both half-spin states through one
noncommuting Lie-algebra path and preserves all four triality invariants. An
18-state real homogeneous spinor cosmology reconstructs a CPL background and
is checked against analytic dilution, density, potential, and Friedmann
identities. Every result is generated from hash-pinned source, checked by two
independent implementations where practical, and reproduced byte-for-byte.

## 1. Scope and claims

The subject is the split real form in eight dimensions. All vector spaces,
matrices, fields, and published numerical outputs are real. The principal
claims are:

1. `Cl(4,4)` is represented faithfully on a real 16-dimensional module.
2. Restriction to the even algebra and to `Spin(4,4)` gives two inequivalent
   real half-spin modules of dimension eight.
3. A split-octonion multiplication table of signature `(4,4)` follows from an
   independently defined Zorn model and satisfies the composition law.
4. The vector module and the two half-spin modules are permuted by an outer
   six-element triality action preserving one trilinear form.
5. The exact tensors can drive reproducible real ODE studies without changing
   the audited CVODE engine.

The work does not claim a classification of cosmological observations, a
microscopic derivation of dark energy, perturbative stability, or a particle
generation mechanism. Those questions require additional mathematics and
data. Historical notebooks are treated as provenance and motivation, never as
proof.

## 2. Real Clifford algebra in split signature

Let `V` be a real vector space with ordered orthogonal basis

```text
(e1,e2,e3,e4,e5,e6,e7,e8)
```

and bilinear form

$$
\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,1,-1,-1,-1,-1).
$$

The Clifford algebra is the unital associative algebra generated by `V` with

$$
xy+yx=2\eta(x,y)1.
$$

In particular, the first four basis generators square to `+1` and the last
four square to `-1`. The normalization factor two is essential; omitting it is
inconsistent with the generator squares.

The standard real classification gives

$$
\mathrm{Cl}(4,4)\simeq \mathrm{Mat}_{16}(\mathbb R),
$$

and

$$
\mathrm{Cl}^{0}(4,4)
\simeq \mathrm{Mat}_{8}(\mathbb R)\oplus
\mathrm{Mat}_{8}(\mathbb R).
$$

Thus the full algebra has one irreducible real module of dimension 16, while
the even algebra has two inequivalent irreducible real modules of dimension
eight. This distinction resolves the most important representation-theoretic
ambiguity in the historical material.

## 3. Canonical integral matrix construction

Define three real 2 by 2 matrices

$$
P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
G=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

They satisfy

$$
P^2=1,\qquad N^2=-1,\qquad G^2=1,
$$

and anticommute pairwise. On the fourth tensor power of the real two-space,
set

$$
\gamma_k^{+}=G^{\otimes(k-1)}\otimes P\otimes
1^{\otimes(4-k)},
$$

$$
\gamma_k^{-}=G^{\otimes(k-1)}\otimes N\otimes
1^{\otimes(4-k)},
\qquad k=1,2,3,4.
$$

The ordered list

$$
(\gamma_1^+,\gamma_2^+,\gamma_3^+,\gamma_4^+,
\gamma_1^-,\gamma_2^-,\gamma_3^-,\gamma_4^-)
$$

consists of integral 16 by 16 matrices and obeys

$$
\gamma_a\gamma_b+\gamma_b\gamma_a=2\eta_{ab}1_{16}.
$$

The 256 ordered monomials

$$
\gamma_{i_1}\gamma_{i_2}\cdots\gamma_{i_k},
\qquad i_1<i_2<\cdots<i_k,
$$

including the empty product, have exact matrix rank 256. They therefore span
the full matrix algebra and prove faithfulness without relying on a dimension
count alone.

## 4. Volume element and half-spin modules

The ordered volume element is

$$
\omega=\gamma_1\gamma_2\cdots\gamma_8=G^{\otimes4}.
$$

It satisfies

$$
\omega^2=1,
\qquad
\omega\gamma_a=-\gamma_a\omega.
$$

The exact projectors

$$
P_+=\frac{1+\omega}{2},
\qquad
P_-=\frac{1-\omega}{2}
$$

both have rank eight. Odd Clifford elements exchange their images, while even
elements preserve them. The spin generators are

$$
\Sigma_{ab}=\frac14[\gamma_a,\gamma_b]
=\frac12\gamma_a\gamma_b\quad(a<b).
$$

They satisfy

$$
[\Sigma_{ab},\gamma_c]
=\eta_{bc}\gamma_a-\eta_{ac}\gamma_b
$$

and the exact `so(4,4)` commutation relations. Restricting the 28 generators to
the images of `P+` and `P-` gives two eight-dimensional modules.

Three independent tests determine their representation type:

- each even-algebra image has rank 64, the full endomorphism dimension of an
  eight-space;
- the commutant of each spin action has dimension one;
- the space of intertwiners between the two actions has dimension zero.

The first two statements prove irreducibility over the real numbers. The third
proves inequivalence for the fixed Lie-algebra labeling.

## 5. Finite-dimensional representation classification

The Lie algebra `so(4,4)` is the split real algebra of Dynkin type `D4`. Its
four fundamental weights may be labeled so that

- `lambda_1` gives the eight-dimensional vector module `V`;
- `lambda_2` gives the 28-dimensional adjoint module;
- `lambda_3` and `lambda_4` give the two eight-dimensional half-spin modules
  `S+` and `S-`.

Finite-dimensional irreducible algebraic modules of the simply connected split
group are indexed by dominant integral highest weights

$$
\lambda=n_1\lambda_1+n_2\lambda_2+n_3\lambda_3+n_4\lambda_4,
\qquad n_j\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}.
$$

The diagram automorphism group fixes the central weight `lambda_2` and
permutes the three outer weights `lambda_1`, `lambda_3`, and `lambda_4`.
Consequently triality acts on every highest-weight family by permuting the
three outer coefficients. The spacetime-relevant fundamental triple is

$$
(V,S_+,S_-).
$$

This statement is more precise than saying that the three spaces are simply
the same. They are inequivalent modules for a fixed labeling; an outer
automorphism changes the labeling and carries one action to another.

## 6. Split-octonion algebra from Zorn data

Write an element as Zorn data

$$
X=(a,u,v,b),
$$

where `a,b` are real scalars and `u,v` are real three-vectors. Define

$$
\begin{aligned}
(a,u,v,b)(c,x,w,d)=(&ac+u\cdot w,\\
&ax+du-v\times w,\\
&cv+bw+u\times x,\\
&v\cdot x+bd).
\end{aligned}
$$

Conjugation is

$$
\overline{(a,u,v,b)}=(b,-u,-v,a),
$$

and the norm is

$$
n(X)=ab-u\cdot v.
$$

The orthogonal coordinates used by the implementation are

$$
\begin{aligned}
a&=x_0+x_4,& b&=x_0-x_4,\\
u&=(x_1,x_2,x_3)+(x_5,x_6,x_7),\\
v&=-(x_1,x_2,x_3)+(x_5,x_6,x_7).
\end{aligned}
$$

They give

$$
n(x)=x_0^2+x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2
-x_4^2-x_5^2-x_6^2-x_7^2.
$$

Exact symbolic verification proves

$$
n(xy)=n(x)n(y),
$$

the left and right alternative laws, flexibility, conjugation reversal, and
the quadratic identity

$$
x\bar x=n(x)1.
$$

In the chosen basis the ordinary multiplication tensor has 64 nonzero
integral entries and rank eight in each tensor mode.

## 7. Para-product and invariant trilinear form

Define the para-product

$$
x\star y=\bar x\,\bar y.
$$

It obeys the same norm-composition law. The trilinear form

$$
T(x,y,z)=\langle x\star y,z\rangle
$$

is cyclic:

$$
T(x,y,z)=T(y,z,x)=T(z,x,y).
$$

This cyclic form is the clean real algebraic carrier of triality. It avoids
choosing one preferred spinor and avoids mistaking a single isometry for the
full three-way symmetry.

## 8. Related triples and split-real triality

The related-triple Lie algebra is

$$
\mathfrak{tri}(\mathbb O_s)=
\{(A,B,C)\in\mathfrak{so}(4,4)^3:
A(x\star y)=Bx\star y+x\star Cy\}.
$$

Each skew endomorphism has 28 parameters, so the initial space has 84
parameters. Evaluating the defining equation on all 64 basis pairs gives 512
scalar linear equations. The exact constraint matrix has rank 56 and nullity
28. Each of the three projections from the nullspace to `so(4,4)` has rank 28.

The three induced eight-dimensional representations have commutant dimensions

$$
(1,1,1)
$$

and pairwise intertwiner dimensions

$$
(0,0,0).
$$

They are therefore irreducible and pairwise inequivalent. Direct intertwiner
calculations identify the second and third projections with the two canonical
half-spin modules from Section 4.

## 9. Outer six-element action

Cyclic permutation of a related triple defines an order-three automorphism:

$$
r(A,B,C)=(B,C,A).
$$

Conjugation defines a reflection that fixes the vector position and exchanges
the spin positions with the required coordinate conjugations. Its exact matrix
has order two. The two operators satisfy

$$
r^3=s^2=1,
\qquad srs=r^{-1},
$$

and generate six distinct automorphisms. Because the order-three operator
permutes three inequivalent modules, it cannot be induced by conjugation inside
the connected spin group. This is the outer triality action.

## 10. Clifford and split-octonion compatibility

Left multiplication by the eight split-octonion basis vectors gives chiral
blocks. Combining each block with its conjugate block yields eight real 16 by
16 matrices satisfying the same Clifford relations as Section 3. The space of
intertwiners between this multiplication-induced representation and the
canonical tensor-product representation has dimension one. A primitive exact
intertwiner has rank 16.

This calculation establishes compatibility among three independently defined
objects:

1. the tensor-product Clifford representation;
2. the Zorn split-octonion multiplication;
3. the related-triple triality representations.

## 11. Numerical study I: 24-state triality transport

Let

$$
y(t)=(v(t),s_+(t),s_-(t))\in V\oplus S_+\oplus S_-.
$$

Choose one time-dependent Lie-algebra path

$$
\begin{aligned}
X(t)=&\frac15E_{01}
+\left(\frac3{20}+\frac{t}{40}\right)E_{14}\\
&+\left(-\frac1{10}+\frac{t}{50}\right)E_{45}
+\frac{t(4-t)}{80}E_{26}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The selected generators do not all commute and include both compact and split
directions. The transport equations are

$$
\dot v=\rho_v(X)v,
\qquad
\dot s_+=\rho_+(X)s_+,
\qquad
\dot s_-=\rho_-(X)s_-.
$$

All three initial states equal the unit basis vector. The exact identities

$$
\langle v,v\rangle,
\quad
\langle s_+,s_+\rangle,
\quad
\langle s_-,s_-\rangle,
\quad
T(s_+,s_-,v)
$$

are invariants of the ODE.

The application uses pure-Rust SUNDIALS 7.8.0 CVODE with BDF, Newton
iteration, a dense linear solver, relative tolerance `1e-11`, componentwise
absolute tolerance `1e-13`, and maximum step `0.02`. Over `0 <= t <= 4`, it
writes 41 samples. The verified run used 237 steps and 251 right-hand-side
calls. The largest measured invariant drift was less than
`3.1e-12`. A second release run produced byte-identical CSV and JSON.

## 12. Numerical study II: real homogeneous spinor cosmology

Use e-fold time `N=ln(a)` and the 18-state real vector

$$
y=(\tau,\rho,\psi_0,\ldots,\psi_{15}),
$$

where

$$
\tau=H_0(t-t_0),
\qquad
S=\psi^T\psi.
$$

The background parameters are

$$
\Omega_{m0}=0.305,
\quad
\Omega_{r0}=0.00009,
\quad
\Omega_{\psi0}=0.69491,
$$

$$
w_0=-0.861,
\qquad
w_a=-0.60,
\qquad
w(a)=w_0+w_a(1-a).
$$

The reconstructed potential is

$$
U(S)=\Omega_{\psi0}
S^{1+w_0+w_a}
\exp\left[-3w_a\left(1-S^{-1/3}\right)\right].
$$

Choose a verified real skew Clifford matrix `J` with `J^2=-1`. The equations
are

$$
\frac{d\tau}{dN}=\frac1E,
$$

$$
\frac{d\rho}{dN}=-3[1+w(e^N)]\rho,
$$

$$
\frac{d\psi}{dN}=-\frac32\psi+\frac{U_{,S}}EJ\psi,
$$

with

$$
E^2=\Omega_{r0}e^{-4N}+\Omega_{m0}e^{-3N}+\rho.
$$

Skewness of `J` gives

$$
\frac{dS}{dN}=-3S,
\qquad S=e^{-3N}.
$$

The continuity equation then gives the analytic CPL density, and substitution
gives `rho=U(S)`. CVODE integrates independently from the present epoch to
`N=-4` and `N=1`, then joins the branches into 1,201 ordered samples. The
verified run used 711 steps and 783 right-hand-side calls. Maximum relative
errors were

| Identity | Maximum relative error |
|---|---:|
| condensate dilution | `6.57e-9` |
| analytic density | `8.67e-10` |
| potential reconstruction | `7.71e-9` |
| Friedmann closure | `3.08e-11` |

Repeated release runs produced byte-identical outputs.

The calculation establishes a homogeneous background realization. It does not
establish perturbative stability. The region with `w<-1` particularly requires
an independent analysis of fluctuations and admissible initial data.

## 13. Reproducibility architecture

The repository separates evidence by ownership boundary:

- `wolfram/` contains exact symbolic definitions;
- `artifacts/exact/` contains hash-linked machine-readable tensors;
- `vendor/sundials_rs` is a read-only Git submodule;
- `studies/` contains application-owned ODE behavior;
- `artifacts/` contains canonical numerical results;
- `notebooks/` contains generated Mathematica and Jupyter presentations;
- `provenance/` contains complete commands for each published artifact.

The exact fixtures are generated twice and compared by SHA-256. Independent
Python programs reconstruct their defining identities. Each numerical study is
run twice in release mode; semantic checkers recompute invariants from CSV and
require byte-identical replay. PowerShell and WSL entry points are both tested.

## 14. Scientific limitations and future work

The exact algebraic conclusions are finite computations over integers and
rational numbers. The numerical conclusions are consistency results for two
specified ODE systems. Neither category supports claims not encoded in its
hypotheses.

Future work should address:

1. global group integration and explicit central-character bookkeeping;
2. orbit and stabilizer classification for null as well as non-null triality
   triples;
3. perturbations of the homogeneous spinor background;
4. data likelihoods with published covariance rather than central values;
5. geometric field equations coupling the split-signature internal structure
   to a physical spacetime model;
6. independent numerical comparison on additional platforms.

## 15. Conclusion

The project establishes, in one reproducible real framework, the exact
relationship among `Cl(4,4)`, its 16-dimensional module, the two real
half-spin modules, split-octonion multiplication, and triality. The
representation distinction is explicit: the full Clifford module is
irreducible, its restriction to the spin group splits, and the three
eight-dimensional fundamental modules are inequivalent until permuted by an
outer automorphism.

The two numerical studies demonstrate how these exact tensors can enter
application-owned CVODE models without changing the solver. Their invariants,
analytic comparisons, hashes, and limitations are all part of the published
result.
